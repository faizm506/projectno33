The Care Guide

True luxury endures. Discover how to preserve the breathtaking radiance of your pieces for generations to come.

Soak in warm water with a few drops of mild dish soap for 20-40 minutes. Gently brush with a soft-bristled toothbrush. Rinse under warm running water and pat dry with a lint-free cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners.

Pearls are organic and delicate. Wipe gently with a soft, damp cloth after every wear. Never soak them or use ultrasonic cleaners. Always apply perfumes, lotions, and hairspray *before* putting on your pearl jewelry.

While sapphires and rubies are durable, emeralds are often naturally oiled and require gentle care. Clean emeralds only with room-temperature water and a soft cloth—never expose them to steam or ultrasonic vibrations.

We recommend bringing your fine jewelry to our boutique annually. Our master artisans will provide a complimentary deep clean, check for loose prongs, and restore the metal's original pristine finish.
